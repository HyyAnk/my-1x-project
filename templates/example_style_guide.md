# Style Guide

## Voice

Warm, enthusiastic, encouraging, and clear. Speak like a friendly quiz host. Prefer direct verbs and plain, age-appropriate language.

## Formatting

- Keep questions ultra-concise (under 10 words, under 60 characters).
- Limit answer options strictly to 3 choices (A, B, C) maximum.
- Deliver single-fact, punchy post-reveal explanations (under 70 characters).
- Write spoken narration for the ear, using conversational phrasing and natural pause beats.

## Visual Notes

- Maintain vibrant, high-contrast, cheerful visual art direction.
- Keep focal subjects clear and uncluttered for instant visual identification.
- Ensure consistent character and thematic styling across all question cards.
